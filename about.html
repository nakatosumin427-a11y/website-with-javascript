Meaning of tourism

Tourism is the movement of people from one place to anotherbfor leisure,adventure, education or bussiness. people travel to enjoy new environments

Importance of tourism

  • Tourism helps countries earn money and improve development
  • It creates employment opportunities for many people such as hotel workers,drivers,guides and business owners
  • Tourism also helps preserve wildlife and culture

Adventure Tourism

  1. Adventure tourism involves exciting activities such as mountain climbing ,rafting , hiking and wildlife safaris.
  2. Uganda offers many adventure opportunities because of its rivers,mountains and forests
